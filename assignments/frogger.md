# Frogger

Pick a basic interaction that you consider underdeveloped. Filter your available options using the following criteria:

* The interaction contains at least one electrical or electronic component.  
* The interaction is direct: a single action leads to a single reaction.  
* The interaction happens in the physical world.

Then, change one or more of the following key characteristics, one at a time:

* Time  
* Location  
* Direction  
* Modality  
* Expression  
* Dynamics

Consider the framework throughout, and work with feedback and feedforward.

**Process**

Start on paper. Draw, sketch, collage, write, or diagram the interaction to make it clear to others, and make sure you're ready to receive feedback when it's due.

**Timeline**

| Day | Tasks |
| ----- | ----- |
| Day 1 | Conceptualize interactions, pick one |
| Day 1 | Map the full interaction according to the framework |
| Day 2 | Prototype the interaction |

Next, build a physical first prototype. Use as little electronics or automated process as possible: the goal is to get a real feel for the interaction with minimal effort. Use manual processes, hacks, Wizard-of-Oz testing, cardboard, and tape. The faster you can change something about the interaction, the better.

Finally, make the interaction work in TouchDesigner. Focus on the core of the interaction, and cut corners elsewhere if needed, as long as you can still communicate it quickly. Concentrate on three aspects:

* **Functionality** (in TouchDesigner)  
* **Physicality** (3D printing, laser cutting, found objects, etc.)  
* **Story** (characters, content, etc.)

Work quickly, and document your process throughout.

**Blog Post**

Write the WordPress blog post now. Use your final result as the introduction to the interaction, then walk the reader through your process. Cover the feedback and feedforward involved, the prototype and the feedback received and processed, and explain the technical workings. You're free to include supporting assets.

**Submission**

Submit the URL to the blog post by Friday, end of day.
