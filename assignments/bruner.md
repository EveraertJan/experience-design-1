# Bruner 

Create an interactive narrative installation designed primarily for children aged 5 to 10\. The story must exist within a clear narrative frame, and must drive a change in the visitor, not just entertain them.

**Process**

1. Conceptualize the story  
2. Sketch  
3. Prototype  
4. Build the installation as far as time allows

**Timeline**

* Wednesday morning: feedback session  
* Wednesday evening: submission  
* On Wednesday, we’ll also discuss the Bruner framework to further refine the story.

**Reference example**  
In a world where the king’s word is law, new rules are announced every few seconds, some of them about kids. News anchors report each new rule as it’s issued. A megaphone stands in the center of the installation, well lit and imposing. When a child yells “no” into the microphone, the installation reacts and the rule making stops, teaching kids that they do have a voice in how the rules that affect them are made.

The accompanying blog post should document the creation process: sketches, iterations, and the final form. The final installation would run as a live TouchDesigner sketch, cycling through audio loops, displaying messages on screen, and reacting to loud noises (ideally transcribing speech and detecting the word “no”).

**Requirements**

* Document everything throughout the process.  
* The final result should work in two ways above all: it must communicate the story clearly, and it must function autonomously.  
* Use TouchDesigner, light, sound (record your own voice where possible), and video.  
* Favor speed of iteration; move quickly.  
* If you’re unsure whether something is technically feasible, ask before spending time on it.

**Submission**  
The blog post should be written so it could also work as a submission for open calls: polished and self-contained enough to stand on its own if entered into a design or art open call, not just read as an internal process log.
