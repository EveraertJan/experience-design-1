# Experience Design I

## About the Course

Within the Experience Design elective track, students learn to design and build hybrid experiences that bring the physical and digital worlds together. We combine image, sound, light and interaction into multisensory experiences with impact.

In Experience 1, we focus on interaction. We conceptualise interactions in the physical world based on frameworks and contextual information, and prototype them through rapid iterations. This way, we learn where interactions truly add value and where they are merely supplementary.

By the end of this course, you will be able to apply interactions deliberately to give your story, identity or experience more impact.

## Course Material

Course material is developed at two moments, so that all information is available to students.

**Before classes start**, tools, materials and sources are compiled to support the learning process. At the start of each lecture, the slides will be available so you can annotate them during the session.

**After each lecture**, the recording is transcribed and turned into a written course text that you can search and consult. This allows you to take in the material in multiple ways. Since there is no theoretical exam, this written course serves purely as support.

## Learning Outcomes (ECTS)

### Interaction

1. The student applies the principles of HCI and related fields to physical interactions and can justify these choices.  
2. The student can conceptualise and design a physical interaction based on the user, the context (physical and societal) and the story.  
3. The student can visualise and communicate the concept of an interactive experience.  
4. The student can develop an interactive prototype of a given conceptual interaction.  
5. The student can translate a story or concept into an experience in physical space.  
6. The student can use interaction to move a story forward.

### Technical

1. The student knows and understands the limitations of different input devices.  
2. The student can use sensors and devices to achieve interaction.  
3. The student can use electronic components to create interactive objects.  
4. The student understands data protocols and can implement them correctly.  
5. The student can use various output devices to present the story in space.

## Examination

The course is evaluated based on a presentation of a portfolio at the end of the semester. The portfolio exists on [experiencedesign.be](http://experiencedesign.be), where students document their works while making them. The presentation and portfolio account for the entire 100% of the grading, including the oral defence.

## Resit (Second Exam Period)

When retaking this course in the second exam period, the submission is the same as in the first: a portfolio that reflects the full course content in an active way (fully developed physical interactions, captured digitally). These interactions are set up on campus towards the end of August for review and evaluation by the lecturers. The exact date will be communicated later.

Please note that the second exam period does not come with the same level of support and feedback as the first.
